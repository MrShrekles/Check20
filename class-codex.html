How To Make A Character
  1. Choose a Class: Begin by selecting a class that will define your character's core abilities.
  2. Select a Path & Talents of your Class: Refine your character's abilities and potential growth with a path and associated talents.
  3. Class Path Progression: Reflects the overall growth in a character's chosen class. It's linear and sequential, with each step unlocking new class-specific abilities.
  4. Talent Progression: Focuses on specialized skills or unique capabilities within a class. Steps within a Talent can be chosen and advanced in any order, allowing for customization and you gain +1 per talent chosen.
  5. Determine your species: Choose a species type for your character and apply the corresponding features to your sheet.
  6. Fill Out Checks (Stats): You have 20 points to put into your checks with a maximum of 10. It's recommended that you put a 10, 5, 3, and 2 with some slight variations.
  7. Roll or Pick from each of these tables: Motivation, Additional Equipment, and a Trinket.
  8. Personalize Your Character: Name, Size (default medium), Gender, Age, Diet, Speed (30ft, +5ft per 2 points of agility), and Gold (1d100 + 50 gp).

Paths & Talents

Path: Represents your character's progression and growth in power. Path progression is linear, unlocking new class abilities in order.

Talents: Customizable skills and abilities that add unique aspects to your character. Talent steps can be chosen from the pool in any order.

Origins

Every path and talent carries an origin - the flavour of power behind it. Origin colours here match the rest of the codex, so a Tech path badges the same colour whether you meet it on a class, a spell, or a monster. Filter by origin to build a themed character, or to see which classes can express a given origin.

Experience & Progression

Each class path has 10 steps, requiring 20 XP per step to advance.
