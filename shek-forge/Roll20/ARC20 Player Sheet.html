Check Result Outcomes

  • 1–4: Fail + Provoke @Adv
  • 5–9: Fail: Provoke
  • 10–14: Fail: Provoke @Dis
  • 15–19: Success
  • 20–24: Success + −1 roll
  • 25–29: Success ++ −2 rolls
  • 30–34: Success +++ −3 rolls

Action Types

  • Move: Up to your Speed
  • Action: The primary activity you perform, like attacking or casting a spell. One per turn unless an ability grants more.
  • Half-Action: Take 2 half-actions instead of a single action.
  • Off-Action: Usable on your turn or after. Provoke, light weapon strikes, switching weapons.
  • Non-Action: Talk, other.
  • Finisher: Eliminate locked or submitted foes.

Actions

  • Attack: Melee or ranged attack. Unarmed deals 1 dmg
  • Grab or Hold: Contested Physical Check (Strength default)
  • Cast Spell: Requires seeing/perceiving the target and having free hands.
  • Administer Potion: Give a potion to a downed ally.
  • Stealth: Attempt to hide for advantage on the next attack.

Half-Actions

  • Stances: Choose a stance to remain in until your next turn.
  • Disarm: Contested Strength or Agility check to disarm a creature.
  • Help: Give a creature +1 to a check
  • Pick up an Item
  • Use an Object: Interact with levers, buttons, etc.
  • Disengage: Move out of a creature's range without provoking.

Off-Actions

  • Provoke: Attack a creature that missed you or willingly left your melee range.
  • Block: Reduce incoming damage by half if you have shield Armor.
  • Demoralize: Contested Influence check; success inflicts Fear.
  • Switch Weapons: Drop a weapon and draw another.

Stances

  • Advantage Stance: Gain advantage on your next attack.
  • Disadvantage Stance: Give disadvantage to enemies attacking you or allies in range.
  • Guard Stance: Provoke without using an Off-Action once when attacked; Provoke at disadvantage on other failed attacks.

Movement

  • Difficult Terrain: Halved speed
  • Jump: 2ft × Agility or Strength
  • Climb/Swim: Counts as difficult terrain
  • Prone: Disadv on ranged attacks; adv for melee attacks against you. Stand costs 15ft
  • Moving Past Creatures: Can only move past smaller creatures
  • Dash: Double distance (Action)
  • Fall: 5 dmg per 10ft
  • Extreme Hazard: 10 dmg/sec
  • Burrowing: Unseen; can't target nor breathe
  • Teleport: Unoccupied space, no opportunity attacks

Special

  • Called Shot: −10 for condition
  • Surprise: Additional action
  • Cover: Disadv. to be hit
  • Full Cover: Cannot be targeted without line of sight
  • Stress Push: Take 4 stress damage to gain a +3 bonus on a low roll.

Size Scale

  • Tiny: <1 ft — pixies, potions
  • Small: 1–4 ft; goblins
  • Medium: 5–7 ft; humans
  • Large: 8–15 ft; ogres
  • Giant: 15–25 ft; siege equipment
  • Monolithic: 25–100 ft; building

Rest

Press On: After combat, recover 1 Class Resource and 1 Mana, repair armor (1d6 + Crafting), and heal (1d6 + Survival). Removes all conditions lasting 1 minute or less.

Long Rest: 10 hours of uninterrupted downtime in a safe environment. Restores all wounds, class resources, and Mana; repairs armor to at least half; removes conditions lasting one day or less. Once per day.
